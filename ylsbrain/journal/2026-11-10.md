### [19:00] Rendered five round-3 concept stills (concepts 6 to 10)   (branch: yls-redesign main · commits: none)

**Synopsis:** The owner leaned toward round-2 concepts 1 (The Pad), 3 (Around the Block), and 4 (Signed, You), and asked for five better mockups. Built and sent five new stills that develop those three ideas. They are renders only, not site pages.

**What worked:** Evidence: `manual:` each final PNG viewed at 1600×1000 after a 96-sample render (`../yls-redesign/tools/concepts/out/concept-6..10-*.png`); the render log printed `done` for c6 to c10.
- Concept 6 Dear ____: macro shot of the pad; one input; the name is written in ink. Concept 7 The Map Table: the pad plus a printed aerial photo, map pins, and yellow cord around the area. Concept 8 Turn the Page: the pad is the site; index tabs are the nav; the corner peels to the next page. Concept 9 Hands On: click any part of the letter or envelope to change it. Concept 10 The One They Open: grey junk mail with one yellow hand-addressed envelope on top.
- New helper file `tools/concepts/lib2.js`. `onQuad` maps an HTML block onto a scene plane with a CSS `matrix3d` homography, so real HTML type sits on a pad in perspective. With `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, scene shadows fall across it.
- `curlPage` bends a plane around a cylinder past a fold line. A `clip-path` on the HTML page along the same fold line keeps the type off the peeled corner.
- A lathe-turned pen nose (brass tip, chrome taper, lacquer barrel) holds up in a macro shot; the kit pen does not.
- Ink drawn on its own canvas, then broken with `destination-out` specks, reads as ballpoint on paper tooth.
- A longer lens (fov 24) and a higher camera (58 to 63 degrees) keep type on the pad readable.
- Concepts 7, 8, and 9 share one desk, pad, and light. They can be pages of one design.

**What did NOT work:**
- `await img.decode()` never resolved in headless Edge with `--virtual-time-budget`; the page stayed blank with no error. An `onload` promise works.
- A syntax or load failure in the page script logs nothing, because the error hook lives in `lib.js`. Check with `node --check` on the extracted script, or open the page in the in-app browser.
- A curled letter corner poked through the envelope that lay on it (concept 9). Flat sheet under an overlapping piece.
- The linen table texture showed tile seams at the origin. `map.offset` moves them out of frame.
- First copy said "3 days to print and post" and "$0.80 each" beside a First Class stamp. `src/content/site.ts` has no turnaround time, and $0.80 is the Standard Class price at 10,000+. Changed to "Real stamp on every piece" and "from $0.80".
